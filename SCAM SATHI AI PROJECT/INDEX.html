High Prevalence UPI & Payments

The UPI "Receive Money" PIN Scam

Scammers pose as buyers on OLX, lucky draw agents, or customer support offering a ₹5,000 refund/cashback and ask you to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code.

Golden Rule: UPI PIN is strictly used for SENDING money from your bank account. You NEVER enter your PIN to receive money!
❌ Scammer Tactics:
  • "Scan this QR code to claim ₹3,000 lottery"
  • "Enter UPI PIN to verify your account for refund"
  • Sending a Collect Request of ₹10,000
✅ What You Must Do:
  • Decline collect requests from strangers immediately
  • Report the VPA (UPI ID) inside your banking app
  • Remember: Money credits directly without any PIN
Surging Vector Malicious APKs

Fake Electricity Bill & Bijli Cut SMS

You receive an SMS: "Dear Consumer, your electricity power will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bill. Immediately contact power officer on 98XXXXXXXX and install update app."

The Danger: The link downloads an `.apk` file (Android package). Once installed, it steals your SMS OTPs and gives scammers full remote access to your phone!
❌ Red Flags:
  • Sent from personal 10-digit mobile numbers (+91...)
  • Asks to download APK files or install AnyDesk / RustDesk
  • Urgent deadline ("power cut at 9:30 PM tonight")
✅ What You Must Do:
  • Never install `.apk` files sent over SMS or WhatsApp
  • Pay electricity bills only via official state Discom apps or portals
  • Verify your bill status directly on your electricity board website
Severe Intimidation Digital Arrest

The "Digital Arrest" & Fake Police Video Call

Victims receive calls claiming a parcel containing illegal passports/narcotics was sent in their name to Cambodia/Taiwan. Fraudsters stage fake police stations on Skype/WhatsApp video calls.

Government Notice: Indian law enforcement agencies (CBI, ED, Police, NIA) NEVER conduct arrests via video calls, nor do they demand money transfers for verification!
❌ Scammer Tactics:
  • Fake uniforms, badges, and forged court arrest warrants
  • Threatening you not to disconnect or talk to family
  • Demanding RTGS transfers to "Supreme Court verification accounts"
✅ What You Must Do:
  • Immediately disconnect the video call
  • Dial 1930 or your local police station to report
  • Remember: There is no legal concept called "Digital Arrest" in India
Persistent Banking & KYC

SBI YONO & Bank Account Blocked Phishing

SMS claims: "Dear SBI/HDFC User, your account is suspended today due to expired PAN/KYC. Click http://sbi-kyc-update.xyz to update within 24 hours."

How it works: The fake website looks identical to internet banking and records your username, password, profile password, and OTP in real-time.
❌ Warning Signs:
  • Links with weird domains (.xyz, .top, .buzz, .info)
  • SMS from unknown +91 numbers instead of official Bank Sender IDs (e.g. SBIBNK)
  • Demands immediate entry of PAN, Aadhaar, and debit card PIN
✅ What You Must Do:
  • Always type your bank URL manually into the browser
  • Banks never ask you to update KYC via third-party SMS links
  • Visit your home branch for any legitimate KYC update
Targeting Youth Jobs & Crypto

Telegram "Like YouTube Videos & Earn ₹5,000" Scam

Starts with a WhatsApp message offering ₹150 for liking 3 YouTube videos or Google Maps reviews. Once trust is built, they invite you to a Telegram VIP group with "pre-paid investment tasks".

The Trap: They show fake virtual balances growing, but when you try to withdraw your money, they demand 30% "GST tax" or "liquidity fee" and freeze everything.
❌ Red Flags:
  • Promises of high daily income (₹3,000–₹10,000/day) for trivial tasks
  • Being shifted from WhatsApp to private Telegram channels
  • Asking you to deposit money to "unlock higher tier returns"
✅ What You Must Do:
  • Never pay money to get a job or task payout
  • Block unknown international numbers (+84, +62, +234, +1)
  • Report fraud Telegram groups immediately
Fast Growing E-Commerce & Postal

Fake India Post / Courier "Address Incomplete" SMS

SMS claims: "India Post: Your package cannot be delivered due to incomplete address. Please update address and pay ₹25 re-delivery fee at http://indiapost-update.click"

The Clone: The ₹25 payment page captures your 16-digit debit/credit card number, CVV, expiry date, and OTP to siphon thousands of rupees!
❌ Red Flags:
  • URL is not indiapost.gov.in
  • Sent to people who have not ordered any postal parcel
  • Payment page asks for complete ATM PIN or card details
✅ What You Must Do:
  • Check tracking numbers only on official indiapost.gov.in
  • India Post never sends random address update SMS with fee links
  • Never enter card CVV or OTP on links received via SMS

🛡️ Golden Rules of Indian Digital Safety

Keep this 4-step checklist handy for parents, grandparents, and students

1

UPI PIN = Only for Paying Out

Never enter your UPI PIN to claim cashback, receive awards, or accept an OLX payment. Receiving money requires zero action from your side.

2

No APK Downloads via WhatsApp/SMS

Never install `.apk` files or remote-control apps like AnyDesk or TeamViewer QuickSupport at the instruction of any caller.

3

Police Never Conduct Video Arrests

CBI, Police, and Customs will never call you on Skype, FaceTime, or WhatsApp to place you under "Digital Arrest". Disconnect instantly.

4

Golden Hour Helpline: Call 1930

If defrauded, immediately report within the golden hour to 1930 (National Cybercrime Reporting) so the bank can freeze the outflow.
