🚨 Live Indian Threat Advisories

Official awareness bulletin for common social engineering tactics.

⚠️ Digital Arrest Video Call Extortion

Scammers dress as police/CBI on WhatsApp/Skype and claim narcotics were found in your parcel, demanding "verification funds". Remember: Indian police NEVER conducts online arrest or bail via video calls.

⚡ Bijli Cutoff APK Disconnection Trap

Fake electricity alerts threaten power cut at 9:30 PM and ask to install an APK or call a private mobile number. DISCOMs never distribute official updates via APK files.

💰 UPI PIN Reverse-Payment Scam

You NEVER enter your UPI PIN to receive money, lottery prizes, or OLX buyer deposits. Entering PIN always debits money from your account.

📱 e-SIM & Customer Care Search Hijack

Fraudulent phone numbers posted on Google Maps pretending to be bank or airline support instruct victims to download AnyDesk or forward SIM activation SMS.
